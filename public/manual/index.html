3 · Productions

Everything hangs off a production: crew assignments, call sheets, budgets, proposals, documents.

  1. New Production → name, client, type, dates, city and primary location.
  2. Open a production to assign crew with per-person call times, link a saved location, and set logistics (basecamp, parking, nearest hospital).
  3. Status moves through Development → Pre-Production → Production → Post → Wrapped.

6 · Locations, Sun Tracker & Director's Viewfinder

Sun Tracker

Golden hour, blue hour, sunrise/sunset, solar noon and shadow direction for any place and date — drag the time slider to see the sun's position and where shadows fall. Works from a search, your GPS, or a saved location. Sun times print automatically on every call sheet.

Director's Viewfinder

Open it on your phone: it simulates the field of view of real sensor + lens combos (Full Frame, Super 35, MFT, Super 16 × 18–135mm) with aspect mattes (2.39, 1.85, 16:9, 9:16) and framing guides. Capture scouting frames — they're saved to the production, tagged with the exact setup.

7 · Documents — 30 production PDFs

Every industry-standard document, pre-filled from your production data: call sheets (with live weather and sun times), one-liner and day-out-of-days, scene breakdowns, department lists, NDAs, crew deals, talent and location releases, risk assessment, catering order, weather contingency, travel & accommodation, deliverables, budget topsheet, purchase orders, invoice tracker, compliance status, treatment and look book — and more. Pick a production, hit Preview or Download.

8 · Budget, Clients & Proposals

Budget & Finance

  • 13 film-standard categories; each line is qty × rate with estimated vs actual and variance totals.
  • Orders tab: purchase orders and invoices with auto numbers (PO-001, INV-001) and status flow Draft → Sent → Approved → Received → Paid.

Clients & Proposals

  1. Add clients to your book (contacts + notes).
  2. Create a numbered proposal with line items and terms; generate the PDF, or hit the link icon.
  3. The client opens the link — no login — reviews the quote and clicks Accept or Decline. The status updates in your panel automatically.

14 · Live Graphics (CG) — Broadcast plan

A real-time broadcast character generator driven by live data: basketball, soccer, NFL, NHL and MLB across 15+ leagues, plus News and Church modes for non-sports shows.

  1. Pick the sport in the sidebar dropdown under Live Graphics, then pick the game.
  2. Every graphic is a button with its own ⚙ — size, team logos, player photos, text scale, texture — per graphic. Pick the player or team before firing.
  3. Preview / Take workflow; anti-collision choreography keeps the score bug, lower thirds and stat graphics from overlapping on air.
  4. Copy the output URL into OBS or vMix as a browser source — that's your key/fill.
  5. Projection mapping: load an image or video, drag the four calibration corners onto the court or field, and it locks in true perspective.
  6. Clock sources: ESPN auto (default), NGSS arena feed, or OES scoreboard hardware for frame-exact clocks.
Note: Live data comes from public third-party sources and official feeds where available. For local teams everything can also run fully manual.
